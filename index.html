About Gleemhop

We are a boutique game studio based in Hong Kong.

Gleemhop is a small, agile team of developers and designers who believe in the power of simple, well-crafted ideas. We focus on creating high-quality casual games that bring a smile to players' faces.

Our process is lean and iterative. We prototype fast, playtest often, and polish until everything feels just right. Every pixel, every interaction, every moment—we craft it with care.
